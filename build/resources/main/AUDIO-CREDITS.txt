Bundled escalator audio — source and license
=========================================================

SmoothLift 随模组分发了 1 段扶梯音效（放在 jar 内
`assets/smoothlift/sounds/audio/subway_escalator.ogg`，由 `assets/smoothlift/sounds.json`
注册成 `smoothlift:audio/subway_escalator`）。它已由作者转换为 Ogg Vorbis 并做剪辑，
玩家直接装模组即可使用，无需任何转码工具。
SmoothLift ships one escalator sound inside the mod jar
(`assets/smoothlift/sounds/audio/subway_escalator.ogg`, registered by
`assets/smoothlift/sounds.json` as `smoothlift:audio/subway_escalator`). It was converted
to Ogg Vorbis and edited by the mod author; players need no conversion tools.

作者对原始录音做的剪辑（因为要循环播放）：
The following edits were made to the original recording (for looping playback):
  1. 原始录音是「11.8 秒声音 + 2.2 秒完全静音 + 9.4 秒声音」，
     剪掉中间 2.2 秒空白与首尾静音（共约 2.8 秒），两段声音拼接；
  2. 拼接处 0.40 秒等功率交叉淡化（crossfade），避免爆音；
  3. 首尾做 0.70 秒等功率交叉淡化（循环点在淡化区内），循环播放无接缝、无停顿；
  4. 未做任何电平（音量）处理，峰值仍为 -23.8 dBFS，与原始录音一致。
  (1) removed the 2.2 s silent gap plus head/tail silence and joined the two takes;
  (2) 0.40 s equal-power crossfade at the join; (3) 0.70 s equal-power crossfade across
  the loop point; (4) no level/gain processing — peak stays at -23.8 dBFS as in the source.
